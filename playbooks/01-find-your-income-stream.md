## Honest model comparison

Ratings are for a solo beginner with no audience. Ranges are what an
ordinary person who keeps going for 12–24 months typically sees. They are
**not** highlight-reel numbers, and the median outcome for every model is close to $0.

| Model | Difficulty to first $ | Ongoing effort at 6 mo | Ceiling | Risk | Realistic range (yr 2) |
|-------|------|------|------|------|------|
| **Templates / tools / presets** (Notion, Sheets, Canva, code snippets) | Low–Med | Low | Med | Low (cheap to try; marketplace dependence) | $0–2K/mo |
| **Ebooks / guides** | Low | Low | Low–Med | Low | $0–1K/mo |
| **Affiliate: SEO content** | High (slow, 6–12 mo) | Med | Med–High | High (Google updates, AI answers eating clicks) | $0–3K/mo |
| **Affiliate: audience-driven** (newsletter/YouTube) | High (needs an audience first) | Med | Med | Med | $0–2K/mo |
| **Micro-SaaS / plugin / extension** | High | Med–High (support, bugs) | High | Med (time sunk) | $0–10K/mo |
| **Newsletter** (sponsors + products) | Med (slow to grow) | Med (must ship weekly) | High | Low–Med | $0–5K/mo |
| **YouTube / podcast** | High | High | High | Med (platform) | $0–5K/mo |
| **Course / cohort** | Med (needs trust) | Med | High | Low–Med | $0–10K/mo |
| **Membership / community** | Med–High | High (not passive) | Med–High | Med (churn) | $0–5K/mo |
| **Licensing** (stock photo/music, frameworks) | Med | Low | Low unless volume/IP is strong | Low | $0–500/mo |
| **Coaching at scale** (group) | Low if you have expertise | High (time-bound) | Med | Low | Funds other streams |

### Proven
- Products that solve a narrow, painful, specific problem outsell broad ones.
- Distribution (audience, SEO, marketplaces) matters more than product quality, up to a threshold.
- Streams that start out time-for-money (services, coaching) are the most reliable way to fund the passive ones.
- Ownable channels (an email list) outlast rented ones (social, search, marketplaces).

### Assumptions to validate for you
- That your particular expertise has buyers online (test by finding people already paying for something similar).
- That you can sustain a content cadence (test with a 4-week sprint before committing).

## Default ranking (no audience, low budget, ~5–10 hrs/week)
1. **This week:** a small paid template or guide built from a skill you already use at work → fastest first dollar.
2. **Months 1–3:** an email list attached to it (lead magnet + newsletter) → the asset that compounds.
3. **Months 3–9:** a higher-priced product (course/toolkit/bundle) sold to that list.
4. **Year 2:** micro-SaaS, membership, or sponsorships, once there's an audience and proven demand.
