# 2. The Passive Income Operating System

Uses intake: all of A, B, C, E.

## Architecture: Asset → List → Ladder → Leverage

```
 Skill ─► Free asset (lead magnet / content) ─► Email list ─► $ product ─► $$ product ─► $$$ / recurring
                    ▲                                                              │
                    └──────────── reinvest profit into distribution ───────────────┘
```

## The 3 stream slots
| Slot | Purpose | Typical fit |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Stream 1 — Fastest to first $** | Proves people pay; funds the rest | Template, guide, or small service productized |
| **Stream 2 — Compounding asset** | Builds owned distribution | Newsletter / SEO library / YouTube |
| **Stream 3 — Highest ceiling** | Grows revenue per customer, ideally recurring | Course, membership, micro-SaaS |

## Digital assets to build first (they compound while you sleep)
1. **Email list + welcome sequence**: owned, portable, and the best long-term multiplier. *Proven.*
2. **3–5 evergreen "pillar" pieces** answering your buyer's top questions. *Proven pattern; how much traffic they bring is an assumption.*
3. **One paid product with a sales page.** *Proven format; demand must be validated.*
4. **A testimonial/case-study bank.** *Proven to lift conversion.*

## Automation baseline (detail in playbook 5)
Checkout + delivery (Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy/Stripe) → email automation (Kit/Beehiiv/MailerLite) → content scheduling → monthly analytics summary.

## Marketing without going viral (detail in playbook 4)
Search-driven content + communities where buyers already gather + partnerships with people who already have audiences. Small and consistent beats viral.

## Reinvestment rule (default)
| Monthly profit | Keep / pay yourself | Reinvest | Buffer |
|-------|------|------|------|
| < $500 | 0–20% | 70–90% (tools, a freelancer, product #2) | 10% |
| $500–$3K | 30–40% | 50% | 10–20% |
| > $3K | 50%+ | 30–40% | 10–20% (6-month runway) |

Reinvest in this order: **time-savers** (automation, a VA) → **distribution** (sponsorships in newsletters, SEO content) → **new stream build**.
*Assumption:* these percentages are a sensible default, not a law. Tune them to your income needs.

## Scaling path (illustrative, using a $29 product / $199 product / $15 membership)
| Level | Stream 1 (templates) | Stream 2 (list/newsletter) | Stream 3 (course/membership) |
|-------|------|------|------|
| **$1K/mo** | ~35 sales/mo | ~1–2K subs, mostly feeding sales | Not yet |
| **$5K/mo** | Bundle + upsells, ~80–100 orders | ~5K subs, sponsors start (~$100–300/issue) | ~10–15 course sales/mo |
| **$10K/mo** | Catalog of 5–10 products | ~10K subs, regular sponsors | ~25 course sales or ~400 members |

The arithmetic is exact; the conversion rates (1–3% of list buying per launch, 2–5% of sales-page visitors buying) are **assumptions** based on commonly reported ranges. Replace them with your own numbers by month 3.
