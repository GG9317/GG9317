# 6. Add More Income Streams in the Right Order

Uses intake: D12–D16, B7, E17–E18.

## Sequencing rule
Each new stream must sell to the **same buyer** or use the **same asset** as an existing one:
1. Core product → 2. Email list → 3. Higher-ticket offer to the list → 4. Recurring offer (membership/SaaS/sponsorships) → 5. Affiliate/partner revenue layered on top.

## Leverage checklist for the next stream
- Existing buyers: pre-sell to them first.
- Existing content: turn it into course modules or a book.
- Existing automations: clone the funnel.
- Existing testimonials: reuse them on the new sales page.

## Risk distribution targets
- No single stream > 60% of income (by year 2).
- No single platform (Etsy, Google, YouTube, Instagram) > 50% of traffic.
- Customer contact data you own (email) for 100% of buyers.

## Time allocation
| Phase | Maintain current | Build new |
|-------|------|------|
| Stream 1 not stable | 100% | 0% |
| Stream 1 stable | 30% | 70% |
| 2+ streams stable | 40% | 60% |

## Reinvestment
Default: 30–50% of each stable stream's profit funds the next build (freelancers, tools, distribution). See playbook 2 for the full split.

## Danger signs: you're expanding too early
- Current stream's revenue swings > 40% month to month.
- It needs you daily (support, fulfillment, posting).
- You can't explain *where* its sales come from.
- You want a new stream because the current one is boring, not because it's stable.

## Stability milestone (green light for the next stream)
**3 consecutive months** of revenue within ±20%, from at least one evergreen (non-launch) channel, with ≤5 hrs/week upkeep.

## 30-day plan template for the next stream
| Week | Goal |
|------|------|
| 1 | Survey existing buyers/list; pick the offer; write the sales page |
| 2 | Pre-sell to the list; target ≥5 buyers or ≥3% of the list |
| 3 | Build v1 (or run it live as a cohort, which is creation and delivery at once) |
| 4 | Deliver, collect testimonials, add to the post-purchase flow of stream 1 |
