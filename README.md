# Passive Income Operating System

A personal, reality-first system for building multiple passive and semi-passive
income streams — built from six strategy prompts and personalized from one intake.

## How it works

1. **Fill in [`INTAKE.md`](INTAKE.md).** One questionnaire replaces the six
   separate question lists (they overlap heavily). Nothing below gets
   personalized until this is done.
2. **Work the playbooks in order.** Each one is a generic, honest framework
   today; each has a `Personalized plan` section that gets filled from your
   intake answers.
3. **Track reality in [`TRACKER.md`](TRACKER.md).** Every assumption gets tested
   and moved to "proven" or "killed".

| # | Playbook | Answers the question |
|---|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | [Find your income stream](playbooks/01-find-your-income-stream.md) | Which models fit *me*, ranked? |
| 2 | [Operating system](playbooks/02-operating-system.md) | What's the full system: assets, automation, reinvestment, scaling? |
| 3 | [Build something that sells](playbooks/03-build-a-digital-product.md) | How do I research, validate, build, price, and launch one asset? |
| 4 | [Get customers without chasing](playbooks/04-customer-acquisition.md) | Which channels, in what order, measured how? |
| 5 | [Automate the boring parts](playbooks/05-automation.md) | What runs without me, and what never should? |
| 6 | [Add streams in the right order](playbooks/06-expansion.md) | When and what to add next? |

## Ground rules used throughout

- **Proven** = widely observed pattern with real-world evidence behind it.
- **Assumption** = plausible, but must be validated *for you* before you bet on it.
- "Passive" is a misnomer for year one. Expect semi-passive: heavy build, lighter upkeep.
- Most people who try this earn little or nothing. The system is designed to
  make failure cheap and fast, so you can afford enough attempts to find what works.
- Nothing here is financial, legal, or tax advice.
